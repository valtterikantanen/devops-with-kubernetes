# Exercise 5.05: Platform comparison

### Introduction

- Rancher and OpenShift are popular Kubernetes service providers.  
- In this comparison, I argue that Rancher is the better option.

### Cost

- Rancher is free and open-source.  
- OpenShift is expensive, especially for enterprise use.

### Ease of use

- Rancher is easy to set up and use with a simple UI.  
- OpenShift setup is more complex and requires more effort.

### Flexibility

- Rancher works with many Kubernetes distributions like RKE, k3s and cloud providers.  
- OpenShift mainly focuses on Red Hat’s ecosystem.

### Community support

- Rancher has a strong open-source community with lots of resources.  
- OpenShift is more corporate-focused and less community-driven.

### Resource usage

- Rancher is lightweight and works well even in small environments.  
- OpenShift needs more resources and is better suited for large setups.

### Conclusion

- Rancher is better because it is free, flexible, and easier to use in different scenarios.
